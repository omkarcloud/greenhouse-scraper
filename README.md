# 💼 Greenhouse Scraper

Greenhouse Scraper is a **free and open-source** scraper that gets you **unlimited** detailed Greenhouse job data for free.

## ✨ What Can I Get?

- 💼 **Every open job at 7,500+ companies** — titles, locations, departments, remote/hybrid & seniority
- 💰 **Salaries straight from the job post** — pay ranges with min, max, currency & period
- 🔎 **Search up to 10 companies at once** — filter by title, location, salary, date & level
- 🔔 **New & closed jobs since any date** — plus application forms, departments & offices

## 📚 Endpoints

10 endpoints cover everything you need.

| Endpoint | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Company Jobs | `/boards/jobs` | Every open job with salary, location, level — filtered and paged |
| Job Details | `/jobs/details` | One job in full: description, pay ranges, custom fields |
| Find Company Board | `/boards/resolve` | A company's Greenhouse board from its name or domain |
| Search Jobs Across Companies | `/jobs/search` | Up to 10 companies searched at once, merged and sorted |
| Company Hiring Overview | `/boards/details` | Open jobs, salary coverage, top departments and locations |
| New & Closed Jobs | `/boards/changes` | Jobs opened, updated or closed since any date |
| Application Form | `/jobs/application-form` | Every applicant question with field types and options |
| Departments / Offices | `/boards/departments`, `/boards/offices` | Org structure as a list or tree, with job counts |
| Education Options | `/boards/education` | Degrees, disciplines and schools the application offers |
